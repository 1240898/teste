# SYS06 - Operational Risk Assessment

## 1. Context & Scope

- **User Story ID:** SYS06
- **Sprint:** Sprint 2
- **Area:** System Administration and Business Continuity (SYS)
- **Responsible:** Mariana Moreira (1240905)


## 2. Requirements & Analysis

### 2.1. User Story Description
> **As a** System Administrator,  
> **I want** the solution's risks identified and quantified,  
> **so that** appropriate mitigation measures can be proposed.

### 2.2. Acceptance Criteria 

- [ ] **AC 1:** Identify between two and six relevant risks in each category: technical, operational, and security. Include risks to personal-data confidentiality, integrity, and availability.
- [ ] **AC 2:** For each risk, identify affected services or information, likelihood, impact, a responsible person, and supporting evidence.
- [ ] **AC 3:** Propose mitigation measures and assess the remaining risk.

### 2.3. Client Clarifications & Questions

- Whether a reference methodology is expected (e.g. ISO 31000 or ISO/IEC 27005), or whether a simplified risk matrix is sufficient.
- Which residual risk level is considered acceptable, and who approves the acceptance of a residual risk.
- Whether the assessment must cover only the DEI integration/demo environment or also the planned production environment.

### 2.4 Goal 

The goal of this user story is to identify, quantify and document the main risks of the LaPrizza solution. Between two and six risks are identified in each category (technical, operational and security), including risks to the confidentiality, integrity and availability of personal data. Each risk records the affected services or information, its likelihood and impact, a responsible person and supporting evidence. Mitigation measures are then proposed and the remaining risk is reassessed, providing input for the business continuity objective (SYS07) and the backup strategy (SYS08).
