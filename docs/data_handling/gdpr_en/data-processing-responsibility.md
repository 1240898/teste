# GDP02 — Data Processing Responsibility

## Controller

**LaPrizza, Lda.** is the controller for the personal data processed through the LaPrizza application. For the purposes of this project, LaPrizza is assumed to be a Portuguese private limited company (*sociedade por quotas*) operating the restaurant and its application. It is the controller because it determines why and how personal data are processed, within the meaning of Article 4(7) of the GDPR. [GDPR][gdpr] · [Portuguese company types][company-types]

## Obligations of the Controller

- **Ensure lawful, fair and transparent processing.** Identify a lawful basis for each purpose. Where processing involves data concerning health, identify an applicable condition under Article 9 as well. Where processing relies on consent, be able to demonstrate that it was given and make withdrawal as easy as giving consent. (Articles 5–7 and 9)
- **Limit processing to what is necessary.** Collect data for specified purposes, keep them accurate and retain them only for as long as necessary or as required by law. Erase or anonymise data once the purpose has ceased and any applicable legal retention period has expired. (GDPR Article 5 and Article 21 of Portuguese Law No. 58/2019) [Portuguese Law No. 58/2019][lei58]
- **Inform data subjects and facilitate the exercise of their rights.** Explain the purposes and lawful bases for processing, recipients, retention periods or criteria, and how individuals can exercise their rights. Handle requests for access, rectification, erasure and other applicable rights, and inform data subjects of their right to lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority (CNPD). (Articles 12–22)
- **Demonstrate compliance and protect the data.** Implement, document and review appropriate technical and organisational measures. Apply data protection by design and by default, restrict access to authorised people and maintain records of processing activities where required. (Articles 5(2), 24, 25, 30 and 32)
- **Oversee service providers and international transfers.** Use processors that provide sufficient safeguards and bind them by a contract or other legal act meeting Article 28. Where data are transferred outside the European Economic Area, comply with the applicable rules in Chapter V. (Article 28 and Chapter V)
- **Respond to personal data breaches.** Document every breach. Notify the CNPD without undue delay and, where feasible, within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach, unless it is unlikely to result in a risk to individuals’ rights and freedoms. Where a breach is likely to result in a high risk, inform affected individuals without undue delay, subject to the exceptions in Article 34. (Articles 33 and 34)
- **Assess high-risk processing and designate a data protection officer where required.** Carry out a data protection impact assessment before processing that is likely to result in a high risk. Consult the supervisory authority before processing if a high risk remains after mitigation. Designate a data protection officer where the conditions in Article 37 apply. (Articles 35–37) [GDPR][gdpr]
